Project
Horizon Europe BIOLEAD, 2026 to 2029

I coordinate strategy on BIOLEAD, a Horizon Europe project building a shared policy roadmap for bio-based solutions and a climate-neutral bioeconomy in Europe. My work sits between researchers, industry and policymakers: turning lab results into positions, partnerships and plans that funders can act on.

Before this I spent five years at the bench and in EU consortia, coordinating work across the EIC Pathfinder project 4DBR and the Marie Curie network ASCTN.

About me

“Deepshika” in Sanskrit translates to “the light of a flame from a lamp”, it signifies the presence of light as knowledge and truly; I derive the greatest joy in pursuing knowledge and using it to create change.

I am passionate about biology and harnessing engineering technology to implement solutions in healthcare and biotech. Broadly, I am interested in science, technology, policy, equity (financial and otherwise) and how these aspects synergestically impact the real world. I have had the opportunity to travel around the world during the course of my training, which informs my interest in understanding cultures. When not discussing science, you can find me sampling different cuisines, painting or reading a book on the way to a nearby hike.

I am also a polyglot of sorts: I speak Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, English and French, and I have A1 in German.

PROJECT IMAGE

Poietis, France · University of Barcelona · May 2021 – 2025

Laser assisted bioprinting of neuronal organoids

  • Marie Curie ITN PhD candidate, Advanced Stem Cell Technologies in Neurology.

PROJECT IMAGE

VU Amsterdam · January – August 2020

Assessing synaptic phenotype of STXBP1 syndrome in isogenic iPSC-derived neurons

  • Patch clamp electrophysiology on STXBP1(+/+) and STXBP1(-/-) iPSC-derived NGN2 neurons.
  • Investigated using immunostaining, protein quantification and confocal microscopy.
PROJECT IMAGE

Charité Berlin · July – August 2019

Establishing RNAscope ISH and detection of Lin41 and FoxJ1 mRNA in mouse development

  • Established the RNAscope method and optimised the workflow.
  • Analysed Lin41 and FoxJ1 mRNA in mouse tissue at E9.5, E14, P1 and P28.
  • Implemented ImageJ macros and streamlined data analysis.
PROJECT IMAGE

Charité Berlin · February – March 2019

Effect of KCC2 modulation on the excitability of rat and human neocortex tissue using multi-electrode arrays

  • Set up MEA equipment in the lab.
  • Tested the action of VU0463271 and CLP257 on neocortical slices.
  • Optimised recording times and characterised ictal-like events and inter-ictal discharges.
PROJECT IMAGE

NIT Warangal · January – May 2018

Premalignant pancreatic cancer detection using mass-spectrometry data by computational methods

  • Compared classification of data using KNN, NNA, DTA and SVM methods.
PROJECT IMAGE

Caltech · Summer 2017

Presynaptic targeting of the ligand in TRACT systems

Khorana Scholarship
  • Tested three presynaptic markers and calculated their efficiency in tracing neuronal connections.
  • Performed confocal microscopy and assisted in molecular biology validation.
